Game Title: Skull Quest

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o3Ja-Zwrvw4

Itch.io: https://n-cooper.itch.io/skull-quest

Genre: 2D platformer

Platform: Windows, Linux

Development time: 6 weeks

Unity Version 6

Course: GAME 360 - Development with Game Engines, Fall 2025

Installation: Extract files, open "Assignment Games" folder and click "Game360_Task3_NickC" executable. The game is built off of task 3 so the executable and many of the files still have that name.

Controls: A to go left, D to go right, Space to jump, and E to shield(Boss level only)
From level 2 onward you gain the ability to wall jump.

IMPORTANT: Click on the text in the victory panel to advance to next level.

Core Mechanics:
- Jumping/Wall jumping
- Shooting
- Special ability(Shield)

Collectibles: Swords scattered around levels

Scenes: title screen, three levels, and a boss fight

Win Condition: Reach the golden skull at the end of each level. Golden Skull in Boss Level is revealed after the Boss is defeated.

Lose Condition: Player health reaches 0

Total Commits: 23

Credits:

Blublu Games for Skeleton assets

MGLAWLESS for environment assets

ALKAKRAB for BGM

B.G.M. for sound effects


Post-Mortem: 
Developing skull quest my understanding of C# has expanded significantly. There were many issues I encountered but through patience I was able to find a solution. I learned a lot about level design,
and gave the levels in Skull Quest discinct atmospheres from eachother using color and environmental details. I also learned about Boss design, and intentionally made the boss fight encourage the use and mastery of
the mechanics.
